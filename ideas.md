# Design Philosophy & Concept: Idrees Khan Portfolio (Redesign)

## Selected Approach: Editorial Creative Tech & Luminous Warmth
- **Design Movement**: Contemporary Editorial & Warm Minimalist Tech (Warm cream/charcoal contrast with vivid vibrant amber/coral and emerald accents).
- **Core Principles**:
  1. *Editorial Sophistication*: High-contrast serif & sans-serif typography pairing (Cinzel/Outfit display headings with Inter body text) for a magazine-quality professional presence.
  2. *Creative & AI-Forward*: Prominently highlight AI Video Generation for Advertisements, Prompt Engineering, and Python Engineering with dynamic interactive skill showcases.
  3. *Organic Flow & Depth*: Soft warm gradient lighting (`#0F172A` deep base with warm amber & emerald glows) creating an inviting, premium tactile experience.
- **Color Philosophy**: Rich dark charcoal/slate canvas (`#0b0f17`) with warm amber accents (`#f59e0b`), emerald highlights (`#10b981`), and pristine white typography.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Magazine-style asymmetrical hero split with overlapping portrait card, interactive Bento grid for projects and expanded AI skills.
- **Signature Elements**:
  1. Interactive AI Video & Prompt Engineering showcase card.
  2. Floating skill proficiency counters & elegant timeline cards for Experience & Education.
  3. Direct CV access and streamlined contact interaction.
